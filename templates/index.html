How a click works

Clicking a window flips it and the windows directly above, below, left and right of it. Diagonals are never touched. Edges and corners flip fewer windows.

How to win

Make all 25 windows dark. Every puzzle can be solved — try to do it in as few moves as you can.

The buttons

  • ◎ Hint — marks a good next window with a moonlight ring. It doesn't change any lights or add a move.
  • ↺ Reset — puts this puzzle back the way it started and sets moves and hints to 0.
  • ✦ New Game — a brand new puzzle with fresh counters.
