How to play

Goal

Turn off every light. yellow windows are ON, grey windows are OFF.

How a click works

Clicking a window flips it and the windows directly above, below, left and right of it. Diagonal windows never change. Windows on the edges and corners flip fewer neighbours.

Winning

When all 25 windows are off, you win. Finish in {{ target }} moves or fewer to earn 10 points. The timer starts on your first click.

Reset

Puts this puzzle back to how it started

New Game

Starts a brand new puzzle
